## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Watermarking guidance can support tracking, invisible marking, or anti-removal workflows that affect privacy and consent expectations.

Mitigation: Use these techniques transparently, with appropriate authorization, notice, and legal review for the deployment context.

Risk: Reference snippets and implementation checklists may be copied into products without validating robustness or user impact.

Mitigation: Review generated implementations before deployment and test watermark behavior against expected content formats, removal attempts, and accessibility needs.
